# 🎓 GrandSchool

GrandSchool is an educational platform for older adults,  
designed to help them stay safe, confident, and informed online.

🌍 https://grandschool.app  
🚧 Currently in development

---

## 🧭 What Is GrandSchool?

GrandSchool offers easy-to-understand online courses that help seniors:

- 🛡️ avoid online scams and fraud  
- 📰 recognize fake news and misinformation  
- 🤖 use artificial intelligence in everyday life  
- 🌐 understand the internet and digital services safely  

---

## 🧓 Who Is It For?

- Older adults who want to feel safer online  
- Families supporting parents and grandparents  
- Communities and social initiatives focused on digital inclusion  

---

## 💙 A Social Impact Project

GrandSchool is built as a social initiative focused on education, safety, and digital inclusion.
